## Version for the mechanism reader (job 1: is the stack sound?)

Between July and October 2026 I put one structural claim about the minimal
self through three registered attempts to measure it: that where a system's integration
genuinely indexes its own centre, deleting the self-locating structure
degrades the act, and where it only describes itself, the same deletion
subtracts a report and leaves the processing intact. The first was a
removal test on an 8-billion-parameter open model, with the stimuli built so
that the referent of identical first-person text is fixed by context rather
than vocabulary, ten adversarially generated controls adjudicated before the
thresholds were locked, and the run executed once. The headline signature
appeared (a 0.219 drop on self-irrelevant integrated tasks, over a matched
control by the registered margin) and the experiment's own pre-registered
turn-boundary control fired on it; two months later I re-read the control's
margin (+0.033, 95% interval −0.133 to +0.200) and relabelled the result as
unable to discriminate routing from a centre, keeping the registered verdict
beside the reassessment. The second built a 30-million-parameter transformer
from scratch on a task that cannot be done without knowing which agent it is;
the ownership input was load-bearing on three seeds (primary battery 0.5683,
0.5633, 0.5738 intact; 0.1988, 0.2015, 0.1447 with the acting channel
zeroed), the registered comparison against a matched non-self control turned
out undefined for every possible model, and the amendment closed as not
testable. The third registered a transplant-ratio measure of how much of the
act is organised around the ownership answer, to be validated on hand-set reference
systems; the pre-stated verification of those systems failed (route use
0.986, 0.000 and 0.024 against a bar of 0.5) and it stopped at its registered
stop. None of the three returned a positive, and each ended in the terms set
before it ran.

The repository is open at the commit I will point you to, with every
registration, threshold lock, amendment, findings file and ruling in git
order, a compute ledger with every dollar and the go it was spent on, and
the nulls filed beside the results: a blind-localization arm that found
own-agent identity nowhere (best margin 1.3 standard deviations against a
bar of 3), an unregistered positive control on a checkpoint where ownership
is known to matter that also found nothing (every probe below its
permutation null), and a fitted linear read at eleven positions and five
layers on three seeds that found the register index nowhere in 135 tests
and whose sensitivity figure I had to correct from one episode in
twenty-seven to one in eleven. The question I would value your read on is
narrow. The constructed line's localization stack, linear probes with
label-permutation nulls on the residual stream, found a real structure on
the 2-billion and 8-billion models and nothing at 30 million, including on
the checkpoint where the lesion shows ownership matters, and the registered
second instrument, causal patching, was never built for that design. Is the
honest sentence "the stack is unvalidated at this scale", as the record now
says, or does what you know about small transformers and belief-state
geometry say that a load-bearing input signal of this kind should have been
findable by these probes at this size, in which case "found nothing" is
evidence about the models and not only about the instrument? What would you
have run, at zero or near-zero cost on the existing checkpoints, before
saying either?

## Version for the claim-scope reader (job 2: does the write-up claim only what it measured?)

Between July and October 2026 I put one structural claim about the minimal
self through three registered attempts to measure it: that a centre of integration cannot
be removed without degrading the integration it centres, while a
self-description can be removed and the processing continues. The first, a
removal test on an 8-billion-parameter open model, produced the signature of
a load-bearing centre and then had it voided by its own pre-registered
turn-boundary control; on re-reading two months later, the control's margin
(+0.033, 95% interval −0.133 to +0.200) spanned zero, and I relabelled the
result as unable to discriminate routing from a centre, reporting the
registered verdict and the dated reassessment side by side rather than
replacing one with the other. The second built a 30-million-parameter
transformer whose self-index was first installed and then, in a registered
amendment, required by the task; the installed one turned out to carry a
constant, the required one was load-bearing as an input on three seeds and
unlocatable as a structure by instruments unvalidated at that scale, and the
amendment closed as not testable because its registered comparison was
undefined for every possible model. The third registered a measure of degree
and stopped at its own pre-stated verification step. The programme's
founding premise, that experience is a sufficiently deep act of temporal
integration marked within the act as happening to someone, was written down
on 2026-09-20, after the first two experiments and a behavioural one had
run, and that date is disclosed in the write-up. On 2026-10-07 the
measurement target moved from the floor, which the project now treats as a
definition and not an instrument, to a two-sided question about which
constructions can produce the indicators of depth an observer perceives and
when observers' detections track them.

The repository is open at the commit I will point you to, with every
registration, ruling, findings file and null in git order and a compute
ledger of every dollar. The question I would value your read on is the one
the external commentary on the global-workspace paper asked of that work:
what does each sentence of this write-up claim, and is it the sentence the
measurement earned? Three in particular. The constructed model's closure
says the ownership input "is load-bearing for the primary battery" and that
"whether an acquired internal ownership structure exists was not
established"; a separate ruling classifies those checkpoints "above zero" on
the project's gradient, in the weak sense that a causally load-bearing
pointer is a centre at the bottom of it, and the write-up carries that
classification only with the ruling attributed. The removal test's relabel
says "the test could not discriminate routing from a centre; the cheaper
account was taken in advance". And the successor's ending says "the built
arms as designed are not references; the measure was not reached". Do any
of these three carry more than the data under them, and in particular does
the "above zero" classification survive the distinction you drew between a
privileged set, a privileged stream and a workspace, or is it the first
being described in words that invite the third?
