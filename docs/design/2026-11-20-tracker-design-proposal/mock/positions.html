Our Working Positions, Against the Evidence

The account argued on Sentient Horizons is scored on the ranking as one theory among the others, by the same rules. This page shows the same evidence the other way round: each public working position, what it said would change its mind, and whether new evidence has gone its way, against it, or neither.

This site ranks its author's own account. So the rules were written before any theory was scored, an outside reader makes the first judgement on every row of this account, and a result against it stands once recorded.
