How far has the science of consciousness got in testing its theories?

Each theory is scored three ways, by rules written down before any theory was scored: how well published studies back it, how sharply it is stated so that a study could prove it wrong, and how much of it no study has touched yet. One of the theories is this site's own, the account argued on Sentient Horizons; it is scored by the same rules, and an outside reader makes the first judgement on every one of its rows.
