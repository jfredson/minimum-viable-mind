# Plainer words for the ranking: draft for John's copy pass (2026-11-21)

*Not applied. Both cold readers found the ranking's jargon hard, and the two readers flagged
different things:*

- *the Claude reader listed "=1", "past the bar" and "support score", and said the column headers
  "need a key";*
- *the Gemini reader said two theories at "=1" with support of 56 and 36 "looks like a mistake
  until you read the fine print".*

*The alternatives below change only words, not the rules or the layout. Every number in them still
comes from the records.*

## The tie

| now | proposed | why |
|---|---|---|
| "=1" beside each tied row | "1" on each row, with the word **tied** under it | "=1" is a sports-table habit; a word says it |
| "Shown as tied: one study going the other way would swap these two." | "**Tied, though their scores differ.** One study going the other way would swap them, so neither is ahead yet." | it answers the Gemini reader's "looks like a mistake" before the reader thinks it |
| "Check it by hand" | "See which study" | says what the link shows |

## The three groups

| now | proposed title | proposed note |
|---|---|---|
| Ranked · "past the bar, with a support score; highest support first" | **Ranked by the evidence** | "clear enough to test, and tested by at least two research groups; best backed first" |
| Testable, too little tested to rank · "no studies yet from two separate groups" | **Waiting for more studies** | "clear enough to test, but not yet tested by two separate research groups" |
| Not yet defined sharply enough to rank · "half or fewer of its core claims come with a prediction that could fail and that a rival theory predicts differently" | **Not yet clear enough to test** | "for half or more of its main claims, there is no prediction yet that could fail and that a rival theory predicts differently" |
| Still being scored | **Not scored yet** | the names, as now |

## The row lines and the column headers

| now | proposed |
|---|---|
| "rests on 3 studies from 3 groups" | "3 studies, from 3 research groups" |
| "needs: a prediction that could fail, and that a rival theory predicts differently, for two more of its four core claims" | "To be ranked, two more of its four main claims need a prediction that could fail and that a rival theory predicts differently." |
| "the evidence so far leans against it" | unchanged |
| How well the evidence backs it (50 = even) | **How well studies back it** (50 means the evidence is even) |
| How sharply it is defined, so it can be tested | **How clearly it can be tested** |
| How much is left to test | **How much is still untested** |
| the phone's labels "backed", "sharp", "left to test" | "backed", "clear", "untested" |

"Core claims" becomes "main claims" in the public text only. The records keep `core_claim`. If John
takes this change, it should be made everywhere at once: the theory pages and About use the phrase
too.

**The weakest step.** "Clear enough to test" sounds softer than the ruled bar it stands for. The bar
is more than half the claims, each with a prediction that could fail and that a rival's own
publication predicts differently. The group note has to carry the full test, or the label promises
less than the rule asks.
