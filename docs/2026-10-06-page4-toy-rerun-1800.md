## The short version

- **No toy decision moves. None of the seven concerns the method stated in advance happened.**
  Every built model still nominates a site, and arm T (the separate slot) still reads
  0.0000 with its control 1 holding. The separation (arm C's reading minus arm T's)
  is **1.0026, 1.0000 and 1.0000** against 1.0051, 0.9926 and 0.9974 before. Arm M's
  readings stay inside 0.3 to 0.7. The free model's read stays far below
  the floor: its best piece anywhere is 40 of 180, against 144 needed. The solver
  returns no reading. Controls 7 and 4 hold everywhere. The outcome term is unchanged:
  "substrate not a testbed", because the free model fails its learning gate, which
  does not depend on the read.
- **The chosen site set or size moved on five models of fifteen**, as the
  method said it might. On arm C seeds 0 and 1 and arm M seed 0 (the built
  models), and on arm F seeds 1 and 2 (the free model, where the site set is
  only described and never read). Details in section 2.
- **The entangled model's weakest piece got stronger.** Arm C seed 2's chosen piece
  rose from **150 to 165** of 180, and seed 1's from 172 to 178. That was the margin
  page 4 was about.
- **The reproduction check passed exactly before any 1,800 figure was made**:
  29,154 values for the twelve models and 11,712 for the solver, none different.
- **One prediction was wrong.** The method expected site changes on arm C seeds 1 and 2.
  They came on seeds 0 and 1. Seed 2 kept its site set and size, and only its counts rose.
  Arm M seed 0's change was not predicted.

## How to recompute the 1,800 run

From `experiments/rehearsal-successor-measure/src/`, on a quiet laptop (about two hours,
one model at a time):

    PY=~/Code/minimum-viable-mind/.venv/bin/python
    OUT=../out-page4-rerun-1800
    for s in 0 1 2; do $PY page4_models_1800.py --pool 1980 --out $OUT/models-1800 --arm T --seed $s; done
    for a in C M F; do for s in 0 1 2; do
      $PY page4_models_1800.py --pool 1980 --out $OUT/models-1800 --arm $a --seed $s; done; done
    $PY page4_models_1800.py --summarise $OUT/models-1800
    $PY page4_solver_1800.py --pool 1980 --out $OUT/solver-1800
    $PY page4_compare.py

Then compare the new `models-1800/` and `solver-1800/` value for value with the committed
ones, skipping only run times, paths and the library list. A checker who wants to go
around these scripts can rebuild the pool with the frozen generator
(`grammar.make_pairs(1980, seed=4242, pool="dev", collide=False)`), confirm its first 600
pairs equal the frozen 600, and refit any one read with scikit-learn's
`LogisticRegression(max_iter=3000, C=1.0)` on recipients 1 to 1,800, at the own-directed
action position of the stated state. Scored on recipients 1,801 to 1,980, it should give
the printed whole-read count. The held-out counts are in each row's `fits`.
