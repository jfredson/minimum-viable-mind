## What was ruled

1. **The named-other condition (page 4).** Both training redesigns failed the
   pre-stated line (curriculum 0 of 3 seeds, loss re-weighting 0 of 3, the
   unchanged recipe 1 of 3; findings section 2). Page 4's fallback (d) stands
   for version 2 of the successor proposal: the registration text records the
   failure, that doubling the budget and both training changes did not fix it,
   and that the staggered first run bounds the money. **Separately, redesign
   (c), a grammar change, is attempted at toy scale on the laptop at $0 on
   2026-09-26**, as its own session, with its pass line pre-stated before the
   run: the named-other condition above the page 1b bar on at least two seeds
   of three on the free toy arm, with the own-directed condition not degraded
   below its current level. If it clears, version 2 is amended before its Gate
   A with the grammar change and the rehearsal items it re-opens (R-1 to R-6)
   re-run; if it does not, (d) is what registers. The findings' diagnosis
   (both action turns receive the same acting signal) is the starting point,
   not a ruling.

2. **Arm M, the fourth arm (page 5).** It passed the pre-stated line
   (chance-corrected readings 0.48 to 0.53 on all three seeds, within 0.04 of
   its construction's prediction; findings section 5). **It is folded into the
   main registration**, under page 5's option (iii) and page 6's $450 envelope:
   three registered runs at the ledger's per-run figures ($32 to $44), which
   with the two releases recomputed from the measured seconds per step
   ($161.90, `docs/2026-09-25-rented-slice-attempt-2-findings.md`) leaves the
   programme at about $434 of $450. The registration describes it as what it
   is: a mixture by item, each action going wholly through the separable or the
   entangled route, about three fifths entangled, not partial separation within
   a trial.

3. **The degenerate-set exclusion (page 3, refined).** Page 1e's exclusion of
   "every layer at every position" is widened: **any site set that spans every
   position at any layer is excluded**, because copying all positions at a
   layer hands the donor's whole forward pass downstream (the rule nominated
   such a set on arm C, seeds 0 and 1; findings section 3). The site count is
   the rule's output (296 on the toy, 1,816 on the registered 12-layer model
   with the toy's position sets); the packet's 176 was hand arithmetic and is
   superseded.

4. **"The smallest layer set that clears it" (page 1c, refined).** The reading
   registered is the one implemented: the smallest layer set that clears the
   four-fifths floor, per position set, then the highest ownership-only share
   among those. The two readings differed on 7 of 12 arm-and-seed pairs by
   0.02 or less (findings section 3).

5. **Control 2.** As the proposal words it (section 7.3, item 2) it cannot run
   on arms T and M, which hold the named agent outside the running state by
   construction, and returns no verdict on an arm that has not learned the
   named-other condition (findings section 4). **It is recorded as not
   applicable on arms T and M**, and on arms C and F it is carried as a
   reported check with its pass line pre-stated, run only once the arm has
   learned the named-other condition, not as a control that holds. Its
   mechanism is not reworded this weekend.

Also ruled, from the findings' "what this means" list: a chance-corrected
reading above 1.0 is reported as observed and not capped (section 6.2); the
