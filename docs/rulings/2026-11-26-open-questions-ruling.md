## 1. The project's aim, and the order of the three hypotheses

**The question.** Should the project's aim change from "The aim is not a system that is provably
conscious" to putting the Sentient Horizons account of experience to removal tests that could
show it wrong? The proposal is `docs/2026-11-20-three-hypotheses-and-refocus-proposal.md`,
section 4. And which hypothesis comes first?

**John's answer:** "Adopt, H1 first".

**What this means.**
- The aim sentence in `CLAUDE.md` is replaced by the proposal's wording (section 4), lightly
  shortened. The last sentence, "The aim is not a system declared conscious", stays.
- The order of work differs from the proposal's. It runs as follows:
  1. **The shared workspace (H1, open question Q10).** It comes first because outside evidence
     already shows the pattern H1 predicts. That evidence is the 2026 study by Wes Gurnee and
     colleagues, in which removing a small, widely shared set of internal contents wrecked
     reasoning and left routine output untouched. It gives a design to start from, and the
     loop test's tools carry over: the removal hooks, the damage-matched random comparisons
     and the margin scoring.
  2. **The split, by taking the speaker cue out of the input (H2, open question Q11).** The
     proposal put this first "because a test exists". But the check of 2026-10-24
     (`experiments/09-split-removal-test/template-coverage-2026-10-24.md`) found that the
     existing conditions do not test this route, so it needs a new design like any other.
  3. **Self-awareness (H3, open question Q12)** comes last.
- The proposal's other two consequences hold as written:
  - the questions about presence in conversation and observers (Q8 and Q9) stay parked;
  - the hibernation from 2027-01-04 still holds, design work is free, and any rental needs
    John's go.

## 2. The speaker-cue test

**The question.** Is a test that takes the speaker cue out of the input worth designing?

**John's answer:** "Yes, after the workspace test".

**What this means.** It is the second test in the order above. Its design starts after the
workspace test is designed. The parts it needs are listed in the 2026-10-24 check, in its last
section. One limit has to be settled in the design: taking the cue away tests whether the model
depends on the cue, which is not the same as removing its own use of the difference.
