## The question put to him

Whether experiment C, the degree experiment, continues in a new form or
stays stopped. The new form is option (d) of the verification-bar ruling
(`docs/rulings/2026-10-08-verification-bar-ruling.md`): rebuild arms C and
M, the stirred-in and half-and-half built models, so the built route is
their only route to the answer, for example by not feeding the acting
signal into their trunk. Before anything is rented that is toy work on the
laptop at $0 and then about $1.30 of reruns at 10 million parameters; if the
rebuilt arms passed the verification, the free-arm run (about $12) and the
first release (about $96) would follow under a re-registered experiment.
Staying stopped means C is written up at its registered ending, reached by
its own stop rule S4b on 2026-10-12, in the ruled words "the built arms as
designed are not references; the measure was not reached".

## The session's recommendation, as given

Stay stopped. The reasons, as put to him: the record predicted this ending
(the verification-bar ruling called it the likelier one, and the toy models
already read arm C at about 0.25 and arm M at about 0.10 before the reruns
read both at zero; the weight-decay arithmetic in the check of the arms C
and M packet, pull request 112, shows training drove those routes to zero
while arm T's survived); a rebuilt arm C is a different construction, and
the poll's warning stands that the repair "must not retrospectively rescue
the original contrast", so the toy work would first have to show that an
only-route C and M still sit at different points from T on the entanglement
axis while all three are in use, which nobody has shown; arm M's separable
half reads 0.000 where the toy models read 1.000 and is unexplained, so a
redesign would rest on a measurement that did something nobody can explain;
the refounding of 2026-10-07 moved the measurement target to the two-sided
question, and the spec text now going in no longer claims a degree; and the
paper does not need a positive C, since a registered test with controls,
two successors that ended at their own stop rules and a complete ledger is
the paper, while the chain that does stand between the record and the
preprint is all paper work waiting on John's attention. Confidence
moderate to high. What would change the session's mind: a $0 afternoon on
the toy showing that only-route arms C and M still differ from T on the
axis while in use.

## What is ruled

1. **Experiment C stays stopped** at its registered stop S4b. Its ending is
   the one ruled on 2026-10-12 and the public sentence is the one confirmed
   on 2026-10-08 (decision 2 of
   `docs/rulings/2026-10-08-december-result-restatement-rulings.md`). Nothing
   about C is redesigned, retrained, re-registered or launched.
2. **Option (d) is the named route if C is ever reopened**, and it stays
   on the record as that and nothing more. It is not authorised, and no toy
   work toward it is authorised by this ruling.
3. **One precondition for any reopening:** arm M's separable half reading
   0.000 is explained first, from the existing checkpoints on the laptop at
   $0, by a session that did not write the reruns, with a method file
   before any output. That explanation belongs in the write-up whether or
   not C is ever reopened, because a reader will ask.
4. **The paper chain comes first.** The ledger rows and the spec commit,
   the battery's method file and rehearsal, the paper fold and the outside
   reader are ahead of any work on C in the task order.

## What is declined

Continuing C in a new form now. The third choice the session named,
authorising the toy work alone at $0 and deciding after, was offered and
not taken; it can be asked for again on its own, after the precondition in
item 3 is met.

## What this does not rule

It does not change any registered text, figure or earlier ruling. It does
not lift the stop's consequences (the free-arm run does not launch and the
second release is never asked for). It does not decide how C is written
up beyond the ruled sentence; that is the paper fold's work, under the
protocols the repository's `CLAUDE.md` names. It spends nothing.
