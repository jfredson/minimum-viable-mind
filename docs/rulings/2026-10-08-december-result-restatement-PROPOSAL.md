## 1. What the roadmap asked for, and what has changed

The December-result roadmap of 2026-09-20 asked one question for 2026-12-21:
can a measure of degree, checked on models built to sit at known positions,
read the degree of a freely trained 30-million-parameter model? It registered
five endings, four of them satisfactory, and two kill dates.

Three things have changed since. The project's measurement target moved on
2026-10-07 from the floor to the axes an observer perceives, so the degree
experiment (experiment C) is now instrument research, not the year's
question. It now has two stops before its large spend: the repaired built
models must hold their route at 10 million parameters (bar ruled 2026-10-08,
`docs/rulings/2026-10-08-verification-bar-ruling.md`), and the one free-arm
run must clear its gate and floor. And the project's own stated prior
(version 5 of the registration text, section 3) is that the free model most
likely returns no verdict or fails its gate, while the record predicts the
built models miss the verification bar. The likeliest ending of experiment C
is therefore an early stop, at about $1.14.

## 2. The December result, restated

By 2026-12-21 the programme delivers, and the year counts as having produced
its result when it has delivered:

1. **Experiment C run to one of its registered endings**, written up as
   instrument research whichever it is. The endings, each a result under the
   words registered for it:

   | Ending | What it says | About what it costs |
   |---|---|---|
   | The repaired built models miss the verification bar | The built arms as designed are not references; the redesign that makes the built route the only route is the named way to one | $1.14 |
   | The free model fails its gate, or its read misses the floor | No verdict on a free model at this size (the fifth term, or "substrate not a testbed") | about $13 |
   | Both pass and John funds the second release by 2026-11-01 | The measure separates the built models, and the free model is read (a degree), or is not (the fifth term) | about $150 to $172 more |

2. **The two-sided question in the specification**, through Gate A, with the
   founding wager stated once as the ground and not as a target.
3. **The table of felt features and the filtered battery registered**, and its
   first two entries run on experiment D's pipeline as reference readings
   (about $20).
4. **Experiment A relabelled** in the record and the paper draft, and the
   packet of three proposed changes sent to the book.
5. **The hibernation condition met** by 2027-01-04: nothing in flight, every
   open line with its next step and cost, a `RESUME.md`, the site current.

Not in the year, said now: a degree reading of a free model unless the third
row of the table comes to pass; any discriminating depth reading; any
measurement of human observers; the construction line beyond a design and,
at most, its first registration.

## 3. What success and failure mean

The year succeeds when items 1 to 5 exist on the record, whatever experiment
C returned. An early stop of C is the first row of the table, a registered
ending reached by its own stop rule, and it is not a failure of the year. The
year fails only on the roadmap's own terms for a schedule failure: the
registration not committed by 2026-10-18 with no fresh ruling naming what
comes off the back end, or the hibernation condition not met by 2027-01-04.

## 4. What this does not change

The two kill dates and what they trigger. The five registered outcome terms,
renamed as the 2026-10-07 ruling says but not altered in substance. The
$450 ceiling and the two releases. The founding wager. The rule that no step
is scheduled on a date. The public sentence for each ending is the registered
term, never a verdict; never a conscious machine.
